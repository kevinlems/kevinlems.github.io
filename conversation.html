02 / The Dinner Format

10 minutes of perspective. An evening of trust.

This is not a seminar. You aren't delivering a 30-minute keynote or pitching your services. That kills the room dynamic. Instead, you spark an intentional conversation, and let peer chemistry do the work.

What We Avoid
  • 30-minute keynote lectures
  • Sales pitch decks & slides
  • Awkward forced calls-to-action
What We Create
  • 10-minute provocative conversation seed
  • High-status peer facilitation
  • Deep human rapport & natural familiarity
Step 01
Arrival & Intimate Welcome
Guests arrive in a private dining space, relax with drinks, and make initial introductions.
Step 02
The 10-Minute Spark
You introduce a compelling macro challenge or trend in your industry to frame the table's dialogue.
Step 03
Peer Discussion
The table weighs in. Guests share their candid insights, revealing their operational realities and pain points.
Step 04
Dinner & Natural Rapport
Conversations continue organically across the meal, cementing genuine personal connection and mutual respect.
The Objective
