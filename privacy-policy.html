Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

1. Data Controller Identification

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and protection of personal information by Occasia Event Services ("Occasia", "we", "us", or "our"). We provide an all-in-one event management, ticketing, and analytics platform accessible via our web application and mobile applications (iOS and Android).

Occasia is committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring full transparency regarding how your data is handled. For any privacy-related questions, you can contact our privacy officer at info@occasia.tech.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our ticketing, event hosting, and account management services, we collect specific categories of personal data when you create an account, register for events, or process transactions:

Email Address

Used for account authentication, ticket delivery, event updates, and customer communications.

Full Name

Used to personalize user profiles, issue event tickets, and identify event organizers/attendees.

Financial Info

Includes payment tokens, billing details, and transaction history required for ticket purchases.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We share data strictly with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our platform, subject to strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Auth0

    Manages secure identity verification, single sign-on (SSO), and account authentication protocols.

  • Stripe

    Processes payment transactions and financial details securely under PCI-DSS compliance standards.

  • Google Cloud

    Provides cloud server infrastructure, database storage, data encryption, and system backups.

4. Account & Data Deletion (In-App Deletion)

App Store & Google Play Compliant

Users can delete their account and all associated personal data directly from within the Occasia mobile application at any time.

How to delete your account from within the app:

  1. 1 Open the Occasia mobile app and log into your account.
  2. 2 Navigate to your Profile or Settings screen.
  3. 3 Tap on Account Security or Delete Account.
  4. 4 Confirm account deletion. Your account, profile, email address, name, and associated data will be immediately and permanently deleted.

Alternative Request Method:

If you are unable to access the mobile application, you can also submit a data deletion request by emailing info@occasia.tech with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". All associated personal and financial information will be permanently scrubbed from our active servers within 30 days.

5. Data Security & Retention

We enforce strict technical and organizational safeguards, including SSL/TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law (such as financial reporting compliance).
